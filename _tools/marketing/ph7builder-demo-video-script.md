# pH7Builder — Screen-Recording Demo Video

**Production script + shot list + voiceover.** Target length: **90 seconds**, horizontal **1920×1080 @ 60fps**.
Every scene maps to a real screen in the software — no invented features.

**How to use this:** record each SHOT from a running pH7Builder install (admin panel + install wizard + front-end).
Read the VOICEOVER over it. Timings are the target; trim to taste. Recommended tools: OBS or ScreenStudio for
capture, a calm mid-pace voiceover (AI or human), soft upbeat background music at ~15% volume.

> Persuasion spine (why it's built this way): **Problem → Speed → Proof → Ownership → Call to action.**
> We open on the entrepreneur's real pain, show time-to-launch (the killer differentiator), prove it with real
> screens, land the trust/ownership advantages competitors can't match (self-hosted, no fees, secure), then ask.

## Music & audio mix

Goal: **upbeat, energetic, engaging, professional** — reinforces the pace without ever covering the narration.

### What track to get
- **Genre / mood:** modern corporate-tech / uplifting electronic-pop. Bright synth plucks, four-on-the-floor kick,
  hand-claps, light arpeggios. Think "startup product launch," not EDM drop and not elevator jazz.
- **Tempo:** **120–128 BPM** (matches the ~1.2s montage cuts in Scene 6 — cut on the beat).
- **Energy arc:** soft/filtered intro (Scenes 1–2) → full groove by the niche/seed demo (Scenes 3–4) →
  peak energy through the features montage (Scene 6) → confident, slightly resolved feel on the CTA (Scene 9).
- **Length:** 90s, or loop/trim a longer bed. End on a clean downbeat, not mid-phrase.
- **Where to source it (royalty-free, commercial-safe):** generate one with an AI music tool (Suno, Udio,
  Soundraful, Mubert), or license from Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Uppbeat (free tier), YouTube Audio Library,
  or Pixabay Music. Search terms: *"upbeat corporate technology"*, *"uplifting startup"*, *"energetic product promo."*
- **If prompting an AI music generator, paste this:**
  > "Upbeat, energetic corporate-tech instrumental for a product launch video. 124 BPM, bright synth plucks,
  > punchy kick and claps, optimistic and modern, builds from a filtered intro to an energetic full mix, clean
  > confident ending. No vocals. Professional, not overpowering — designed to sit under a voiceover."

### The mix (this is what keeps music from burying the narration)
- **Voiceover:** the loudest element. Target around **-3 to -6 dB** peaks.
- **Music bed:** keep it **12–15 dB below the voiceover** whenever narration is playing.
- **Ducking (do this):** apply **sidechain / auto-duck** so the music automatically dips ~10–12 dB the instant the
  voiceover speaks and rises back in the gaps between scenes. Every editor has it: Premiere "Auto-Duck,"
  DaVinci Resolve Fairlight ducking, CapCut/Descript "duck background music." This single step is what makes it
  sound professional instead of amateur.
- **Let the music breathe:** in the pure-visual moments with no narration (the Scene 6 montage beats, the CTA card),
  bring the music **up ~6 dB** so the energy carries the cut — then duck again when the voice returns.
- **Loudness target:** master the final mix to about **-14 LUFS** (the standard for web/YouTube) so it's punchy but
  not clipping.
- **Polish:** 0.5s fade-in at the very start, 1s fade-out under the final CTA.
